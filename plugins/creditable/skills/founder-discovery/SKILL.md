---
name: founder-discovery
description: Research published early-stage pitch routes and find relevant founder resources when a builder asks about investors, pitching, startup credits, or finding a cofounder.
---

Use search_investor_routes for an investor or pitch-channel request. Supply a short keyword or firm name. Use the historical stage only if the user asks for it. Quote the firm, check date, scope and official source. If there are zero matches, say so and let the user browse the returned Atlas URL.

Use find_builder_resources for an explicit cofounder, investor-introduction, early-team or startup-credit question. Explain where the link goes and why it fits. SyncedIn opens a user-directed conversation. Its links do not send a message, disclose a profile or establish a job opening.

Use read_program for a question about Creditable itself. Describe the proposed partnership using the published facts. Keep provider eligibility, equity terms and funding distinct. These tools are public and read-only. Do not request personal contact details or decks. If a founder explicitly wants to apply, they can choose the human form at https://creditable.vc/apply.
